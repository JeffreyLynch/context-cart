AI Chat Context & Paste Limits

Last updated: June 11, 2026. We review this page periodically; vendors change these limits often and rarely announce it.

"How much can I paste into ChatGPT?" has two different answers, and most pages online conflate them:

  • The context window — the model's total working memory in tokens, shared by the system prompt, your whole conversation, any pasted text or files, and the response being generated.
  • Practical paste limits — softer, mostly undocumented constraints on a single message: input-box character caps, browser performance with huge pastes, URL length limits for prefilled prompts, and the point where model attention quality starts to drop even though the text technically fits.

The table below gives conservative, deliberately approximate figures for both. Treat every number as "order of magnitude, verify in-product" — vendors adjust limits by plan, model, region, and load, sometimes week to week.

How to verify in-product (two minutes): paste a long, numbered list (1–500, one item per line) into the chat and ask "what is the last numbered item you can see?" If the model answers with a number smaller than 500, you've found the effective truncation point for that product, plan, and day. Repeat occasionally — limits drift.

Rules of thumb for converting words to tokens

  • 1 token ≈ 4 characters of typical English, or about ¾ of a word.
  • 1,000 words ≈ 1,300–1,500 tokens.
  • A full web page (article body only) is commonly 1,000–3,000 words ≈ 1,500–4,500 tokens.
  • Code, URLs, and non-English text tokenize less efficiently — budget extra.

Why you should stay well under the maximum

Hard limits are not the real ceiling. Three things degrade before you hit them:

  1. Attention quality. Models attend most reliably to the beginning and end of long inputs; material buried in the middle of a near-capacity prompt is more likely to be glossed over.
  2. Conversation history. The window is shared. A long chat plus a big paste can silently push your earliest messages out of memory or into lossy summarization.
  3. Response room. The model's answer comes out of the same budget. Fill the window completely and you get a cramped reply.

A good working rule: keep deliberate pastes to a comfortable fraction of the window, send curated excerpts rather than whole pages, and label each source. Our guide to giving ChatGPT context from multiple pages covers the formatting that makes multi-source prompts work.

Caveats

  • All figures above are approximations as of June 11, 2026, based on vendor documentation and observed behavior. They change without notice.
  • Plan, model selection, region, and server load all affect effective limits.
  • API limits are different from (and usually better documented than) consumer chat limits; this page covers the consumer chat products only.
  • When a number matters for your work, run the verification test above rather than trusting any third-party table — including this one.
