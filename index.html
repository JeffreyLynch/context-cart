Collect snippets from the whole web.
Send them to ChatGPT in one shot.

Context Cart is the local-first research cart for ChatGPT and Claude: clip text from many pages over hours or days, keep every source attached, review the composed prompt, and send the bundle in one go — no account, no server, no credits.

How it works

Highlight text and press a shortcut, or right-click any page, link, or selection. Snippets accumulate into named local carts, each one tagged with the page title, URL, and timestamp. When you're ready, review the composed prompt in the side panel and send it to ChatGPT — or copy it for Claude.

Features

🛒

Carts with provenance

Organize snippets into named carts. Every snippet keeps its source page title, URL, and capture timestamp — so your AI conversation always knows where each piece came from.

⚡

Right-click + shortcut capture

Capture a selection, a link, or a whole-page reference from the right-click menu, or grab the current selection instantly with Alt+Shift+A. Add manual notes in the side panel too.

📝

Saved prompts + preview

Store reusable prompt templates and always see the exact composed message — sources, snippets, and instructions — before anything is sent. No surprises.

🚀

Send to ChatGPT, your way

Open a fresh ChatGPT draft, drop the bundle into your current conversation, or enable autosubmit to fire it off in one click. Each mode is opt-in and clearly explained.

💬

Claude support

Prefer Claude? Copy the composed prompt to your clipboard with claude.ai opened for you, or hand it straight to the Claude Desktop app for review before sending.

⬇️

Markdown export

Export any cart as clean Markdown with headers per source — perfect for notes apps, docs, or pasting into any AI chat that isn't directly supported.

✓Privacy first — and verifiable

Context Cart is local-only by design. Not "we promise we don't look at your data" — there is simply no place for your data to go.

  • Local-only storage. Everything lives in your browser's local extension storage. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into, nothing to be locked out of.
  • No credits or quotas. The extension never meters your usage, because there is no backend to meter it.
  • No analytics, no telemetry. Zero tracking of any kind.
  • Zero network calls. The extension makes no network requests of its own — you can verify this yourself in the DevTools Network tab. Data only leaves your browser when you send a prompt to ChatGPT or copy it for Claude.
